50 of the 50 Oasis parts have no cost on file

The Oasis rule pays price − cost. With the cost left at zero the margin becomes the whole selling price, so those parts are paying out their full price today.

Fill in the cost for each Oasis part and the payouts in the table below drop to the real margin automatically. Kent and Aquaguard are unaffected — they pay a percentage of the price and never look at cost.
