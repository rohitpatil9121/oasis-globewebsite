Oasis Globe · Technician payouts

How incentive is calculated

Nothing here is stored or entered by hand. Every rupee is worked out from closed jobs, the parts on each bill, and the parts catalogue — recalculated fresh each time the screen is opened.

The four numbers everything runs on

Branded rate

6%

Of the price the customer paid, on Kent and Aquaguard parts.

Branded rate — bonus

10%

Replaces 6% for the whole day once the daily target is reached.

Daily target

₹10,000

Total billed by that technician in one day, IST.

GST cut

18%

Taken off the Oasis margin when the job was paid online.

The part people miss

Incentive comes only from parts. A day of pure service calls with no parts fitted earns ₹0 in incentive, however many jobs were closed.

Service charges still matter though — they count toward the ₹10,000, so they help unlock the 10% rate on the parts that are fitted.

The formulas

A Kent or Aquaguard part

payout = price × quantity × rate rate is 6% or 10%

Straightforward percentage of what the customer was charged. Our cost does not come into it.

An Oasis part

margin = (price − our cost) × quantity never below zero payout = margin paid in cash payout = margin × 0.82 paid online — 18% GST removed

The technician keeps the whole margin on our own parts. When the customer pays online the company owes GST on that sale, so 18% comes off the margin first.

Cash or online?

online if (money taken online) > (money taken in cash) otherwise cash

Decided per job, not per part. UPI, card and “online” count as online; everything else is cash. If a job has no payment recorded yet it is treated as cash, so an uncollected job never quietly underpays.

The daily rate

day billing = total of every closed job that day rate = 10% if day billing ≥ ₹10,000, else 6%

Worked out once for the whole day and applied to every job in that day, including ones closed before the target was reached.

Which jobs count

RuleDetail
Only closed jobsA job counts once its status is CLOSED. Open, assigned and in-progress jobs earn nothing yet — they appear the moment they are closed.
Indian calendar dayDays run on IST, not UTC, so the ₹10,000 window matches the working day rather than cutting off at 5:30 in the morning.
Which day a job lands inThe payment time is used. If there is none, the work-done time; then the close time; and only failing all three, the day the request came in.
Per technicianThe ₹10,000 is each technician’s own billing. Two technicians on ₹6,000 each do not unlock it for either of them.
Unknown partsIf a part is not in the catalogue it has no brand, so it pays nothing. A part fitted but never added to the catalogue silently earns ₹0.
