Oasis Globe · Technician payouts

How incentive is calculated

Parts, brands, daily ₹10,000 targets and GST cuts no longer decide payout. Every closed call pays a fixed amount for that technician, then the customer’s feedback multiplies it.

The two numbers everything runs on

Per call

Fixed ₹

Set per technician from experience. A senior tech can be on ₹80, a newer one on ₹40 — whatever the office enters.

Bad

×0

No incentive on that call.

Average

×1

The fixed amount, as entered.

Very Good

×2

Double the fixed amount.

The formula

payout = per-call amount × multiplier Bad multiplier = 0 Average multiplier = 1 Very Good multiplier = 2 not yet rated multiplier = 0 — held until the customer replies

Bill size, parts fitted, cash vs UPI, and how many jobs were closed that day do not change the rupees. Two technicians on the same call count can still earn different amounts because each has their own per-call rate.

What this replaces

The old Kent/Aquaguard percentage, Oasis margin, 18% GST cut and ₹10,000 daily bonus are retired. A day of service-only calls now earns the same as a day of membrane jobs — quality of the visit is what moves the payout.
