01 Customer says only “hi” Fixed text · no AI

The most common opening. This reply is hard-coded word for word, so the four lines can never come out short or reworded.

Customer

Hi

Bot

Hi. This is Oasis Globe water purifier service department. Please share: – Name: – Issue: – Address: – Photo of purifier.
No AI callRecognised as a bare greeting and answered directly. Costs nothing and replies instantly.
Why fixedThe AI kept dropping the “photo of purifier” line, so the text is sent verbatim from code.

02 Customer sends all details at once AI

Most people copy the template back with their answers filled in. Name, problem and address in one message is enough — the request is raised immediately.

Customer

Gaurav Patil Water filter blink not working Flat 4, Ashwini Apartment, Vishal Nagar, near Shell petrol pump, Pune

Bot

Gaurav Patil, your service request has been logged. Ticket ID: OG-300726-0007 Service Issue: Water filter blink not working Address: Flat 4, Ashwini Apartment, Vishal Nagar, near Shell petrol pump, Pune We will assign a technician and update you here.
Behind the scenesThe AI looks the customer up, opens a request, saves the name, address and problem, then submits it — five steps in one go.
Photo is optionalThe bot never waits for it. If it arrives, it is attached to the request.
Confirmation is fixedThe ticket number block is written by code, not the AI, so the format never varies.

When the bot deliberately stays quiet

SituationWhat happens
A manager takes over the chatThe AI is switched off for that customer from the dashboard toggle. It stays off until switched back on — nothing turns it off automatically.
A technician messages the business numberRecognised as staff. The message is saved so the office can reply, but no request is raised and the AI does not answer.
The same message arrives twiceWhatsApp sometimes redelivers. Duplicates are dropped, so the customer never gets two replies or two tickets.
Customer sends several messages quicklyThe bot waits about a second after the last one and answers all of them together, instead of replying line by line.
Everything is taking too longAfter 30 seconds the bot stops and sends “One moment — our team is checking this and will reply here shortly”, rather than leaving the customer waiting.
