Service reminder ka poora flow

Purane clients ko WhatsApp pe "6-monthly service due hai" ka message kaise jata hai, client ke jawab pe kya hota hai, aur wo jawab dashboard me kaha dikhta hai.

Neeche ki screens dashboard ki nakal (mock) hain, asli screenshot nahi — naam aur number banavati hain, taaki ye page kisi ko bhi bheja ja sake aur kisi client ka number bahar na jaye. Layout, buttons aur wording wahi hai jo live dashboard me hai.

Rasta 1 — Marketing page (haath se)

Purani billing list ke ~4,662 clients. Owner clients chunta hai aur Send dabata hai. Template: service_reminder_general. Ye page isi raste ke baare me hai.

Rasta 2 — Automatic

Jo client dashboard se service karwa chuka hai, use ticket close hone ke 6 mahine baad apne aap message jata hai — machine ka naam aur last service date ke saath. Roz max 20, sirf 10am–6pm. Koi button dabana nahi padta.

Uske baad, aur yaad rakhne wali baatein

6 mahine baad wapas

Reminder sent wala client 6 mahine baad apne aap All clients me laut aata hai, agle reminder ke liye.

Kharcha

Har message ka Meta charge leta hai: Utility ~₹0.12, Marketing ~₹0.8–0.9 (approx). Meta me template ki Category dekh lo — wo khud badal sakta hai.

Dheere shuru karo

Pehle sirf apne number pe test. Phir 50–100 roz. Zyada log block/report karein to Meta number ki quality gira deta hai — isi number se ticket wale messages bhi jaate hain.

List me non-customers hain

Billing software ki poori party list hai — suppliers, "Enterprises", "Services" jaise ~237 naam. Bhejne se pehle dekh lo, unhe select mat karo.

Address kyun nahi hai

Reminder ke liye chahiye nahi. Jo Book Service karta hai, bot usse address le leta hai — data apne aap poora ho jata hai.

Kaun bhej sakta hai

Marketing page sirf owner login ko dikhta hai. Manager ya technician ko nahi.
