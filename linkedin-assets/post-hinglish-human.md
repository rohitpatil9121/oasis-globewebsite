# Post 1 — Hinglish (human version)

---

6 mahine pehle ek RO service centre ke owner se baat hui thi. Unki problem sun ke laga ye toh har chhote business ki kahani hai.

Customer WhatsApp pe complaint bhejta tha. Owner ya manager phone pe technician ko batate the. Kabhi kabhi bhool jaate the. Customer 2 din baad phir message karta - "bhaiya koi aaya nahi". Technician bolta mujhe bataya hi nahi. Sab pareshan.

Maine bola isko fix karte hain.

Ab unka system aise chalta hai - customer bas "hi" bhejta hai WhatsApp pe. Bot naam address problem puchta hai, ticket ban jaata hai. Manager ke dashboard pe turant dikhta hai, wo ek click me technician assign karta hai. Technician ke phone pe app hai jisme pura job flow hai - accept karo, nikle toh customer ko message chala jaata hai, pahunche toh GPS se proof, TDS reading dalo, estimate banao.

Aur sabse important cheez - jab tak customer WhatsApp pe estimate approve nahi karta, koi paid kaam start nahi hota. Owner ka khud ka rule tha ye. Bole customer ko surprise bill kabhi nahi jana chahiye.

Ek aur rule tha unka jo mujhe shuru mein ajeeb laga - customer ko har step pe sirf EK message jaye, spam bilkul nahi. Baad mein samjha ki yehi trust banata hai. Ab unke customers khud bolte hain ki Urban Company jaisa feel aata hai.

Stack ke liye Node.js, Supabase aur Meta ka WhatsApp API use kiya, dashboard React mein hai aur technician app Android APK. Details comment mein daal dunga jo puchhna chahe.

3 mahine se live hai. Ek bhi inquiry miss nahi hui ab tak.

Freelance kaam karta hoon. Agar aapka business bhi WhatsApp aur diary pe chal raha hai toh DM karo, baat karte hain.
