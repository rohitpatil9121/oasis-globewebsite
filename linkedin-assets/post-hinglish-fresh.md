# Post 1 — Hinglish (just-finished version)

---

Kal raat ko ek RO service centre ke owner ko unka naya system handover kiya. Aaj subah unka message aaya - "pehli complaint aayi WhatsApp pe aur dashboard pe khud ticket ban gaya". Us message ka feeling alag hi hai.

Kahani ye thi - unka pura business WhatsApp aur phone calls pe chalta tha. Customer complaint bhejta, manager phone pe technician ko batata, kabhi kabhi bhool jaata. Customer 2 din baad phir message karta ki bhaiya koi aaya nahi. Technician bolta mujhe bataya hi nahi. Roz ka jhagda.

Pichle kuch mahine isko fix karne mein lage.

Ab aise chalta hai - customer bas "hi" bhejta hai WhatsApp pe. Bot naam address problem puch ke ticket bana deta hai. Manager ke dashboard pe live dikhta hai, ek click mein technician assign. Technician ke phone pe app hai jisme pura job flow - accept karo toh customer ko message, nikle toh message, pahunche toh GPS se proof, TDS reading, estimate.

Aur ek cheez jo owner ki khud ki demand thi - jab tak customer WhatsApp pe estimate approve nahi karta, koi paid kaam start nahi hoga. Bole customer ko surprise bill kabhi nahi jana chahiye. Unka doosra rule - har step pe sirf EK message, spam nahi. Shuru mein laga simple si baat hai, baad mein samjha ki pura notification system isi ke around design karna pada.

Stack - Node.js, Supabase, Meta ka WhatsApp API, dashboard React mein, technician app Android APK. Detail comment mein.

Ab dekhna hai real customers ke saath kaise chalta hai. Jo seekhne milega wo bhi share karunga.

Freelance kaam karta hoon. Aapka business bhi WhatsApp aur diary pe chalta hai toh DM karo.
